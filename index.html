Business Challenge

Many small retail businesses have trouble managing their stock. This causes losses and makes it hard for them to grow. The Smart Retail Dashboard System was built to help solve that problem for business owners.

Solution

This system helps business owners track their stock digitally, record their sales, and monitor their expenses. It reduces errors and makes it easier to manage the business day to day.
