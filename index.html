Smart Retail Dashboard System

This is a system that works in a web browser so users can organize products and monitor what they sell.

Home Inventory Sales

Business Challenge

In small retail companies, owners frequently find it difficult to organize their supplies and follow their daily transactions. As people record information by hand, they are likely to make errors, lose their data and choose actions for the business that do not lead to success.

To fix those issues, the Smart Retail Dashboard System is available.

Solution

By using the Smart Retail Dashboard System, people access a digital tool that allows them to organize items and observe how many units remain without manual effort. On this platform, the software records transactions in a way that is precise and uses browser localStorage to hold data.
