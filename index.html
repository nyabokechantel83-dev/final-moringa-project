Business Challenge

Many small retail businesses struggle to manage their stock. This leads to losses and makes it hard for them to grow. The Smart Retail Dashboard System was created to help solve this issue for business owners.

Solution

This system allows business owners to track their stock digitally, record sales, and monitor expenses. It cuts down on mistakes and simplifies daily management.
